# Privacy Policy

Aura is a privacy-focused, offline-first task manager.

- **No data collection:** Aura does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.
- **Local task storage:** Tasks, notes, subtasks, lists, and settings are stored on the device using SwiftData and UserDefaults.
- **Local widget sharing:** Aura's app and widget share a small progress snapshot through an Apple App Group on the same device.
- **Notifications:** Reminders and optional daily briefings are scheduled locally with Apple's UserNotifications framework.
- **No tracking:** Aura has no analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking.
- **No account required:** Aura does not require registration or a cloud account.

Your information remains under your control on your device.
